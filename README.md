# Confession Booth for Atheists

*A small, quiet place to say the thing out loud, even if you don't believe anyone is listening.*

## English

### Why I made this

This started with *Fleabag*. Moriarty was very compelling in that scene which inspired me into making this little side vibe-coding project.

At the time, my own life was unsteady. I was anxious, and everything felt like it was moving faster than I could keep up with. I didn't have a place to put all of it.

Around then I had a neighbor who studies theology, and she shaped how I think about this more than she probably knows. Through her I started to see confession differently. It isn't really about being forgiven by someone above you. It is about naming what weighs on you, and finding out that it gets lighter once it is spoken. You don't need to believe in anything in particular for that to be true.

So I built a confession booth for people who don't believe in one. It was meant to make me feel a little better, and to make my friends laugh and think a little. I am happy to say it did both.

### What it does

You walk into a dark church, breathe, and choose what weighs on you:

- something you did
- something you left undone
- something you carry

You say a short prayer, type your confession, and watch your words turn into stars. They drift, answer back with a gentle reply, and finally gather into a single light. Then the page tells you:

> We listen and we don't judge. We've heard your prayers.

There is no right way to use it. Nothing here asks you to believe.

### A note on privacy

Confessions are saved anonymously (date, category, and text only) to a Google Sheet. Nothing identifying is collected, but please **do not write names or personal details** in the box. If you would rather nothing be stored, remove the `saveToDatabase(...)` call in `index.html`.

### Run it yourself

It is a single static page, so it works on GitHub Pages as is.

1. Fork or clone this repo.
2. Keep these files in the same folder as `index.html`:
   - `background.png`: the church image
   - `music.mp3`: the background music
3. (Optional) Replace the SheetDB URL in `saveToDatabase` with your own, or remove it.
4. Enable GitHub Pages in **Settings → Pages**.

### Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Cormorant Garamond and Cinzel from Google Fonts.

## 中文

### 为什么做这个

灵感来自《Fleabag》。莫里亚提很帅。

做这个项目的时候,我的生活一团乱麻，我仿佛被困在毛线里的猫，只能无助地尖叫，以祈求四脚兽的帮助。

那段时间,我总是去找学神学的邻居乞讨（不是），一起吃饭，我们总是谈一些有的没有的，我们一起逛超市，那是我最开心的时候。

于是我做了一个给"不信教的人"用的忏悔室。最初是想让自己好受一点,也想随手做个好笑的vibe coding的东西。

### 它是什么

你走进一座昏暗的教堂,先深呼吸,然后选择压在你心上的是哪一类事:

- 我做过的事
- 我没来得及做的事
- 我一直背着的事

接着念一句祷词,写下你的忏悔,看着自己的文字变成星星。它们飘散开来,页面会给你一句温柔的回应,最后星星汇聚成一束光,屏幕上出现:

> We listen and we don't judge. We've heard your prayers.

这里没有正确的使用方式,也不要求你相信任何东西。

### 关于隐私

忏悔内容会匿名保存到一个 Google 表格,只记录时间、类别和文字,不收集任何能识别身份的信息。但还是请**不要在输入框里写真实姓名或个人信息**。如果你不想保存任何内容,删掉 `index.html` 里的 `saveToDatabase(...)` 调用即可。

### 自己部署

这是一个纯静态页面,直接放在 GitHub Pages 上就能运行。

1. Fork 或 clone 这个仓库。
2. 确保 `index.html` 同一目录下有:
   - `background.png`:教堂背景图
   - `music.mp3`:背景音乐
3. (可选)把 `saveToDatabase` 里的 SheetDB 地址换成你自己的,或者直接删掉。
4. 在 **Settings → Pages** 里开启 GitHub Pages。
